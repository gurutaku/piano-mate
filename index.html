1Build your music

Drag up or down to choose each pitch on the grand staff. Place notes in the same column to make a chord, or in separate columns to make the next notes.

Page 1
Drag on either staff to place notes. Use the small arrows to change pages. Notes in the same column make a chord; tap a notehead to remove it.

Microphone settings & tips

Allow microphone access in Safari. Place the phone near the piano, but not directly against its speaker. Play each note clearly and let it ring briefly. The detector can occasionally miss very soft notes or hear room noise.
